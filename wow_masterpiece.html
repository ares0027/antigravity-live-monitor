What are the lead times for custom architectural fabrication?

Standard serialized fixtures ship within 14 business days. Custom dimensional pendants engineered for high-ceiling auditoriums or bespoke metallurgy require 6 to 8 weeks for precision CNC milling, hand-finishing, and cryogenic optical calibration.

Can LUMINA systems interface directly with Lutron HomeWorks and DALI-2?

Yes. Every LUMINA pendant integrates native multi-protocol transceivers supporting DALI-2, DMX512-A, Art-Net 4, sACN, and native 0–10V analog dimming with zero flicker down to 0.01% current threshold.
